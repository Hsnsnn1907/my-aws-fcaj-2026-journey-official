On this page, you will need to introduce your worklog. How did you complete it? How many weeks did you take to complete the program? What did you do in those weeks?

Typically, and as a standard, a worklog is carried out over about 3 months (throughout the internship period) with weekly contents as follows:

**Week 1:** [DevOps, CI/CD, Git, Docker, and Architecture](./1.0-week1/)

**Week 2:** [FCAJ Familiarization & AWS Setting up](./1.1-week2/)

**Week 3:** [EC2, IAM & Establishing First Instance](./1.2-week3/)

**Week 4:** [Deploy Codebase & Exploring Infrastructure](./1.3-week4/)

**Week 5:** [Pushing Rules & Microservices Planning](./1.4-week5/)

**Week 6:** [Branch Switch (feat/CI-CD) & Cleanup](./1.5-week6/)

**Week 7:** [Designing the CI/CD Pipeline Flow](./1.6-week7/)

**Week 8:** [Audit Resolution & Fixing 6 CI/CD Blockers](./1.7-week8/)

**Week 9:** [Fixing Deployment Pipeline & Build Issues](./1.8-week9/)

**Week 10:** [E2E CI/CD Verification over EC2 Host](./1.9-week10/)

**Week 11:** [Implementation of Monitoring & Hardening](./1.10-week11/)

**Week 12:** [Project Knowledge Transfer & Retrospective](./1.11-week12/)
