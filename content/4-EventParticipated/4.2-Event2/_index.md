Event Name: GenAI-powered App-DB Modernization workshop

Date & Time: 09:00, August 13, 2025

Location: 26th Floor, Bitexco Tower, 02 Hai Trieu Street, Saigon Ward, Ho Chi Minh City

Role: Attendee

### Event Objectives

The event was structured to share best practices regarding modern application design while introducing Domain-Driven Design and event-driven architecture to all participants. It sought to provide technical guidance on selecting the exact right compute services specific to workload needs. A core focus was to present various AI computing tools that can be directly applied to accelerate and support the entire software development lifecycle.

### Speakers

The presentations were led by Jignesh Shah, acting Director of Open Source Databases, alongside Erica Liu, Senior GTM Specialist for AppMod. In addition, Fabrianne Effendi, Associate Specialist SA in Serverless technologies at Amazon Web Services, provided deep technical insights.

### Key Highlights

Initial discussions centered around identifying the fundamental drawbacks of legacy monolithic application architectures, demonstrating how long product release cycles invariably lead to lost revenue and missed market opportunities. Presenters showcased how transitioning to a modern modular system drastically improves efficiency by breaking functions into independent services that communicate safely via events. 

A thorough outline covered Domain-Driven Design methodology through identifying domain events, arranging accurate timelines, establishing acting entities, and defining strict bounded contexts appropriately linked to bookstore case studies. Technical focus also shifted heavily toward Event-Driven architectures, detailing publish and subscribe configurations alongside real-time streaming integrations, thereby enforcing resilience and extreme scalability. Finally, a significant spotlight was dedicated to Amazon Q Developer, revealing how AI capabilities completely automate code transformation strategies, ranging from simple Java scaling up to profound database modernization deployments.

### Key Takeaways

I gathered crucial technical considerations focusing on the business-first approach which emphasizes starting designs heavily oriented toward business domain workflows rather than just standalone technology. Utilizing ubiquitous language creates a foundational bridge mapping the communication seamlessly between development and stakeholder teams. Event storming techniques emerged as a highly practical method to safely transition synchronous network calls into robust asynchronous events. Modernization inherently requires a heavily phased adoption strategy to completely minimize runtime risks, validating that adopting tools like Amazon Q Developer within the pipeline leads directly to extensive reductions in return-on-investment timelines.

### Event Experience

Attending the GenAI-powered App-DB Modernization workshop was extremely valuable, giving me an extensive and comprehensive view covering how to safely modernize applications and databases using sophisticated tools. Gathering direct insights from highly skilled experts at AWS introduced an immense depth of theoretical knowledge alongside applicable practical blueprints.

Participating hands-on with technical exposures related to event storming substantially helped me visualize and formulate logical solutions detailing how business processes convert directly into domain events. Exploring tools spanning Amazon Q Developer inspired ideas tailored to enhancing code workflows extending from pure initial planning all the way down to final production maintenance. Overall, the networking and extensive discussions opened channels connecting with diverse domain peers, significantly helping me reshape my forward-thinking application designs toward an asynchronous future.
